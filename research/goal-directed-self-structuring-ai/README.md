## Relationship to Root Engineering

Root Engineering remains a complementary persistence architecture:

> The AI may continuously redesign the path. Root Engineering preserves validated state that must survive those redesigns.

GDSSA should therefore not be interpreted as a Root Engineering subfeature. Root Engineering is one possible supporting architecture inside a broader goal-directed self-structuring system.
